These controls are examples, not part of the library. Each demo is one file: open its source,
copy it into your game and restyle it. They are built from the same atoms as the library widgets:
`SilkGraphics`, `Spring` and `damp`, signals, `trackGesture` and `RadialMenu`.

## Spin button and autoplay

<Demo name="slot-spin" height={320} caption="Tap to spin, tap again to stop. Hold for the autoplay picker and drag to a count." />

The arrow morphs into a stop square while the reels run. The yellow ring shows the autoplay spins
left. Holding opens a half `RadialMenu` in marking mode, so press, hold, drag and release picks a
count in one gesture.

## Balance, win and bet

<Demo name="slot-wallet" height={340} caption="Turn the dial to pick a bet, press SPIN. Tap a counting win to skip it." />

Built on the library's `Odometer` and `JogDial`. The dial's detents are positions on a bet
ladder, and its readout shows the bet at that position.
