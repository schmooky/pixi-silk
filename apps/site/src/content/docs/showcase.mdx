Whole screens built with pixi-silk. They are site code, not library code, so copy the parts you
need. Each demo opens in Studio with its source.

## Design sheets, rebuilt 1:1

Each pair shows the reference image and a still frame of the live render. Open a sheet to drag a
compare slider over it. The widget viewer and the experiments listed at the end of this page open
any single widget full screen.

## Dashboards

<Demo name="weather-mono" height={720} caption="Switch the theme." />

A weather board in two tones. It uses hairlines, discs and one monospace face. The needle, the
compass and the sunrise arrow move on their own layer, so the rest is drawn once.

<Demo name="finance-bento" height={720} caption="Hover a tile." />

Each tile is a small container with its own drawing. Lift any one of them into your app as it is.

<Demo name="spectra-hud" height={460} />

The heatmap is about 700 cells in one `SilkGraphics`. It redraws at half the frame rate.

## Light scenes

These use `pixi-silk/glow` and `pixi-silk/glass`. [Glass and glow](/docs/glass-and-glow/) explains
the pieces.

<Demo name="glass-prism" height={440} caption="Drag to move the light. Change the spread and the frost." />

<Demo name="glow-sunrise" height={440} />

A red disc under a large blur, and a yellow shape whose blur fades out towards a crisp horizon.
The fade is a progressive blur, covered in [shadows and effects](/docs/shadows-and-effects/).

<Demo name="glow-beams" height={520} />

The beam is a pinched path drawn three times with less blur each time. The wave is a white stroke
with red, orange and blue copies a few pixels off. The bolt is one shape cut into strips, each
moved sideways.

<Demo name="glow-aurora" height={470} caption="Change how high the light rises and how fast it drifts." />
