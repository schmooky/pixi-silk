This page is for translators. You work in a spreadsheet, and a build step turns it into the text
the game draws. You never need to install anything.

## The sheet

Each row is one message. Fill only the columns of your language.

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `key` | The message's name. Never change it, never delete it. |
| `context` | A note for you: where the text appears and what the placeholders are. |
| `slots` | The placeholders and their kind. Leave it as it is. |
| `maxLen` | The longest the text should be, in characters. |
| `ar.other`, `ar.few` and so on | Your text, one column per plural form. |

Your language column names use the game's own codes, such as `iq` for Iraqi Arabic or `kz` for
Kazakh. Your producer tells you which column is yours.

## Placeholders, tags and objects

- `{name}` is a value the game fills in, such as a player name or a number. Keep every placeholder
  of the English text, with the same name. You may move it anywhere in the sentence.
- `<gold>1,250</gold>` is a style, such as gold coloured text. Keep the same tags. Put them around
  the words that carry the meaning in your language.
- `<coin/>` is an icon. Keep it, and place it where it reads well.
- To write a brace or an angle bracket as text, put a backslash before it: `\{`, `\<`.

## Plural forms

A message with a number has one column per plural form your language uses. Arabic has six forms
(`zero`, `one`, `two`, `few`, `many`, `other`), English two, Japanese one. Fill every form your
language has. The build reports a missing one, and it ignores forms your language does not use.

## Excel habits that break text

Format your columns as text before typing. The build reads exactly what the cell holds and stops
on these mistakes, with the sheet, the row and the column:

- A cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` becomes a formula.
- `007` becomes the number 7, and `1/2` becomes a date.
- Invisible characters pasted from other programs are removed, with a warning. Right-to-left marks
  and no-break spaces are common ones. Write `<nbsp/>` where you want a no-break space.

Smart quotes, ellipses and line breaks (Alt+Enter) are fine.
